## 1. Goal

Customers scan the QR code in the Fidara box, register once, work through 10 puppy-training courses, and can send one video to Charlotte for a personal review. Sign-ups are saved where the team can use them, including for the Estoy Aquí membership.

## 5. Known limits (by design, for now)
- The admin dashboard reads the same files as everything else; no database is needed for it.
- Customer data lives in one JSON file on the server's disk. Fine for a box product. Move to a database if volume grows or you need automations.
- Anyone who has a customer's personal link can open their courses. It is a private link, not a password.
- If a customer registers a second time with the same email, they get no new link. They must use the email we sent. (Deliberate: otherwise anyone who knows an email could take over the account.)
- Customer sign-up emails are sent only when SMTP is configured.
- Video lessons are placeholders until you provide them; the audio plays meanwhile.

## 6. Do not change
- Upload field names: `video, lesson, lessonName, name, email, dog, note, receipt, duration`. (An optional `X-Fidara-Token` header was added alongside them.)
- The box promise: record, press Send, review emailed back.
- URL paths `/#/lesson/N` and `/qr.html` once the domain is final.
- `LESSONS` order in `server/server.js` matches `public/lessons.js`.
