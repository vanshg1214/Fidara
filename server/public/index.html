Questions

Good to know

Do I have to go in order?

We suggest it, because each course builds on the one before. But nothing is locked, so you can open any course at any time.

How does the video review work?

Your box includes one video review from Charlotte. Record up to 5 minutes on any course page, add what is hard and press Send. She emails you what to adjust.

How do I get back in, or use another phone?

When you register we email you a personal link. Open it on any phone and your courses and progress come back. You can also add Fidara to your home screen so it opens like an app. Keep the link private, since anyone who has it can open your courses.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone, and on our server once you have registered, so the personal link in your email can restore it on another phone.

The camera is not working. What now?

On any course page you can use your phone’s own camera app, or choose a video you already have. Both options sit under the recorder. The live camera needs a secure (https) page and your permission.

What is Estoy Aquí?

Your box includes a free membership to the Estoy Aquí neighborhood pet community. Registering signs you up, and Estoy Aquí will email you to finish setting it up.
